Close the case: verify gaps are resolved or explicitly accepted, the
report exists in `reports/`, hypotheses are open/confirmed/refuted (open ones
are listed to the analyst with a warning), then set `case.status=closed`
through mcp-case (gated, explicit confirmation). Journal the closure. French
output. Closing never deletes anything; evidence stays immutable.
